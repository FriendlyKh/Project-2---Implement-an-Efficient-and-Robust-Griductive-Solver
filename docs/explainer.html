Act 2 · The Logic

How do we "know for sure"?

We give each person a propositional variable.

CAlice = True → Criminal
CAlice = False → Innocent

Now every clue can be written as a logical formula.

Act 2 · The Logic

Clues become logic.

"Exactly 1 criminal among {Alice, Bob}" becomes:

English ↓ Logic
(CA ∧ ¬CB) ∨ (¬CA ∧ CB)

Either Alice is the criminal, or Bob is — but not both.

Act 2 · The Logic

What's CNF?

Conjunctive Normal Form — a formula written as an AND of ORs.

Think of it as puzzle pieces that must ALL be true.

CA ∨ CB
∧
¬CA ∨ ¬CB

Clause 1: "At least one criminal."   Clause 2: "At most one criminal."
